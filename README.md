# shree-ji-store-sales-dashboard
The dashboard provides a clear overview of sales, profit, quantity sold, product categories, customer segments, and time-based performance. It helps identify top-performing products and categories, understand sales trends, and evaluate overall business performance.


## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Excel Sales Dashboard developed for Shree Ji Store to analyze sales performance, profitability, product categories, and business trends.

## Business Problems Addressed

- Monitoring overall sales and profit performance
- Identifying top-performing product categories
- Comparing sales performance across different periods
- Identifying profitable and low-performing products
- Understanding sales trendsThe dashboard provides a consolidated view of sales and profitability and helps identify important business trends and performance areas.


## Key Features

- Sales analysis
- Profit analysis
- Category-wise analysis
- Product performance analysis
- Interactive filters
- KPI indicators
- Trend analysis

## Business Impact

The dashboard helps management quickly understand business performance, identify areas requiring attention, and make data-driven decisions.
